Eight ideas for the lid of your laptop. Each one gives a stranger an easy reason to say hello: a question they can ask back, a line they can read from across a table, or a QR code for people who won't interrupt you.

Printing

Getting them made

Any shop that does die-cut vinyl stickers can print these.

Material

Matte white vinyl. Gloss shows glare under café lights and is harder to read from an angle.

Cut

Die-cut with a 3 mm white border, as shown. Kiss-cut sheets work too if you want all eight on one page.

How many

About 50 of the hero, so you can hand them out. Ten or so of each of the others.

Files

Once you pick, I'll export print-ready files at 300 dpi with the text turned into shapes, so the fonts print exactly.
